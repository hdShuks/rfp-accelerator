Item 1. Business

Acme Robotics Inc. designs and manufactures autonomous warehouse robots for mid-market logistics operators. We generate revenue primarily through hardware sales and multi-year software subscriptions. Our largest customers are regional third-party logistics providers in North America and Western Europe.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business depends on a small number of contract manufacturers concentrated in Southeast Asia. Supply chain disruption, tariff changes, or component shortages could materially harm our results. We face intense competition from larger, better-capitalized incumbents.

Item 1B. Unresolved Staff Comments

None.

Item 2. Properties

We lease approximately 220,000 square feet of manufacturing and office space.

Item 7. Management’s Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Revenue increased 25% year over year, driven by expansion within existing accounts and improved subscription attach rates. Gross margin expanded 180 basis points on manufacturing efficiencies. We expect continued investment in R&D.

Item 7A. Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk

We are exposed to interest rate risk on our variable-rate debt.
